# END Statement

<!-- keywords: END -->

Ends a program, or a block.

## Syntax

`END`
`END` { `IF` | `SUB` | `FUNCTION` | `SELECT` | `MODULE` | `TRY` | ... }

## Comments

Alone, `END` stops the program. With a word it ends the block that word started. QuickVB writes the `END` of a block for you when you press Enter after its first line.

## Example

```vb
END
```
